## Recommended positioning

**Primary title: Applied AI Engineer.**

This is a positioning recommendation based on the responsibilities in the sampled postings and Sawab's supplied experience—not a claim that this title has the highest search volume or guarantees the highest salary. It describes building useful AI-powered systems without restricting the work to chatbots, one automation tool, or one industry.

- **Forward Deployed Engineer:** strongest supporting role family for customer discovery, hands-on implementation, integration, and delivery. Keep it visible in the About section, experience, and hiring call to action.
- **AI Solutions Architect:** relevant secondary search target when the job is hands-on. Some architect openings require substantially deeper cloud specialization than the current resume establishes; do not imply that every architect role is a match.
- **Business process automation:** a valuable capability and outcome, not the primary job title. Keep the 300+ automations and operational improvements as evidence.
- **Machine Learning Engineer / Research Engineer:** not the primary positioning. Model training, ML infrastructure, and research expertise must not be inferred from building LLM applications.
- Do not add Senior, Staff, Principal, or Head of AI solely because a high-paying posting uses that level. Keep historical employment titles unchanged.

## Evidence gaps worth addressing next

Do not silently add these as current skills just to match postings:

1. **Evaluation evidence:** a reproducible test set, baseline, failure categories, before/after results, and ongoing monitoring. Laurel and Appsilon emphasize this; an “AI evaluation” skill alone does not demonstrate it.
2. **Production reliability evidence:** document actual latency/cost trade-offs, retries, permissions and incident handling where personally implemented. Distinguish implemented controls from plans.
3. **MCP / specialist frameworks:** SPsoft asks for MCP; some roles ask for LangGraph or observability products. Add only after confirming hands-on work or completing a demonstrable project.
4. **Deep cloud / ML infrastructure:** Kubernetes, GPU serving, fine-tuning, PyTorch, AWS/CDK and advanced Azure architecture are not established by the current resume. Treat as role-specific gaps, not keywords to manufacture.
5. **Engineering proof:** a shareable implementation, architecture decisions, testing and measured outcomes will strengthen positioning more than another list of tools. Use sanitized or independent work; do not publish former-employer code or confidential data without authorization.
